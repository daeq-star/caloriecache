## Calorie check

These stayed on the board with a calorie-check badge. The badge means the density looks off, not that the kit was dropped.

- ReadyWise 1 Month ($279.99, 62,540 kcal) and 2 Week ($149.99, 25,580 kcal) ship with Shopify weights that work out above 4,000 kcal per pound. Pure fat is about 4,080 kcal per pound, so the weight or the calorie total needs a second look.
- 4Patriots Fruit, Veggie & Snack kit states 3,920 calories and 120 servings, about 33 kcal a serving. That is under the 40 kcal floor this site uses as a sanity check.
